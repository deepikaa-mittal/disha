DISCLOSURE
Dataset Scope

Derived from 2,132 laboratory epochs across controlled stimulus protocols. Findings represent experiential associations and require multi-cohort longitudinal study.

Feature Derivation

Signals are feature-derived reconstructions from extracted FFT spectra, covariance matrices, and eigenvalues, rather than direct raw clinical microvolt telemetry.

Career Aptitude Boundary

EEG and gameplay metrics observe real-time performance style and stress pacing. They do NOT measure innate personality, IQ, or deterministic occupational aptitude.
